Effective Study Strategies

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals to improve long-term retention. Use this technique especially for difficult concepts.

Pomodoro Technique

Study in focused 25-minute intervals with short breaks in between to maintain high productivity and avoid burnout.

Active Recall

Test yourself regularly rather than passively rereading material. Create flashcards or practice questions.

Teach to Learn

Explain concepts in your own words as if teaching someone else to solidify your understanding.
